Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense Policies

1. Client-Side Tool Processing

MCP Tool Schema Builder operates as a 100% client-side web utility. All schema parsing, Zod code generation, FastMCP Python formatting, and JSON-RPC serialization occur directly inside your web browser’s local memory via JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, or store your parameters, API tokens, or business code on any remote server.

We may partner with third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our website.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data. Because we do not collect personal identifiers, emails, or personal databases through the utility tool, no user profiling is performed.

4. Log Files and Infrastructure

Like most web hosting platforms (such as Hostinger / Cloudflare CDN), standard web server access logs are automatically created to record incoming requests (such as your IP address, browser type, and timestamps). These logs are utilized solely for server health monitoring, security audits, and DDoS protection.
